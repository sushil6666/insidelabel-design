# InsideLabel (copy of the Lovable preview app)

Snapshot of the InsideLabel app as shown in the Lovable preview, copied on 19 October 2026.

It reads printed ingredient lists from a photo or pasted text for packaged foods, skincare and personal care products, and medicines. Food checks are live today (additives, hidden sugars, ultra-processing, front-of-pack claim checks); skincare and medicine lists are read back while those checks are built. It also has compare, search, your scans, household and an admin area for approving claim rules.

This folder is a copy. It is separate from the Python/FastAPI app in the rest of this repository and needs its own settings before it runs: copy `.env.example` to `.env`, fill in the values, then `bun install` and `bun run dev`.
