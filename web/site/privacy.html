Privacy

Beta version, last updated 6 October 2026. A lawyer will review it before the public launch.

What we collect

  • An install id. A random number your browser makes the first time. No name, phone number or email.
  • Your invite code, only if you joined with one, so we know which tester sent a scan or a comment.
  • Photos of packs you take or upload, and the barcode.
  • For each household member: an age band (for example "toddler") and their avoid list (for example milk). Nicknames you type stay on your phone and are never sent.
  • Feedback and reports you choose to send. "Report a problem" also sends what the app did in the last few minutes (screens, requests, errors), and shows you exactly that before sending.

Who reads your photos

Google reads the label for us: Gemini reads the ingredient list, and Google Cloud Vision reads the text again as an independent check. We use Gemini's paid service: under Google's terms, Google doesn't use what we send to improve its products, and keeps it for a limited time only to detect misuse and meet legal requirements. Our reviewers look at photos of packs that aren't in our catalogue yet, or that you sent to us. Please photograph only the pack.

How long we keep it

  • Your scan photos are deleted 30 days after the scan, unless you reported a problem with that scan, sent the pack to us, or the photo became the reviewed record of a pack in our catalogue.
  • After 30 days, each scan loses its link to your install id and the household details sent with it (age bands and avoid lists). We keep what the label said, and on which days an install scanned. A scan you reported, or sent to us, keeps its link while we work on it, and for at most 12 months.
  • Feedback and problem reports are deleted after 12 months.
  • Your install id, any invite code and agreement to the terms are deleted 12 months after the app was last used on that device.
  • Daily backups keep deleted data for up to 14 more days.

Children

InsideLabel is for adults. When you add a child to your household, you are asked for consent as their parent or guardian; only their age band and avoid list leave the phone.

Your choices

In the app, Your data (at the bottom of every screen) shows this phone's id and has Delete my data. It deletes your photos, feedback, problem reports and any invite link, and removes any link between your scans and this phone. A pack you helped add to our catalogue stays in it, with nothing that points to you.

To ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or with any question or complaint, write to connect@insidelabel.ai and quote your phone's id. We answer within 90 days. If you aren't satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
