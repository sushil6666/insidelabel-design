InsideLabel

See what's inside the pack

Scan the barcode or photograph the ingredient list of Indian packaged food. InsideLabel tells you what's in it, starting with anything on your family's avoid list.

Open the app

InsideLabel is in a small, invited beta. If you have an invite, open the link from your message.

How it works

  1. 1. ScanThe barcode, or a photo of the ingredient list on the back. The front of the pack is optional.
  2. 2. ReadAn AI reads the words on the label. It doesn't judge anything.
  3. 3. CheckFixed rules, based on FSSAI regulations, check each ingredient and your family's avoid list.

What it is, and isn't

  • It states ingredient facts, quoting the label. It isn't medical advice: for a health condition, ask your doctor.
  • Allergens come first. When a photo is hard to read, it says it couldn't confirm, and asks for a clearer one.
  • Every pack in our catalogue was checked by a person against photos of the real pack.
  • Brands can't pay to change or hide what InsideLabel shows.
